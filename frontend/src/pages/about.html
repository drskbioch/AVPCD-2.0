About

Release 0.1. The records are curated. Their accuracy is known only as a range, and records outside the benchmark sample have not been checked by an independent person. A benchmark of 150 records read by two independent field experts, with no third reader, puts the share of correct fields between 95.7% and 99.2% and the share of records with every checked field correct between 78.6% and 96.9% (stratified to the benchmark frame; 95% intervals in the release README, supplementary file S6). The column method_as_recorded repeats the curation method string of the workbook; it describes how a record was produced and is not evidence of independent checking.

What the records are

Each record is one antiviral potency value (IC50, EC50, IC90, EC90 or ED50) of one plant-derived compound against one virus in one assay, copied from one paper. The release holds – records from – papers. They are the headline scope of a larger curation workbook of 1,988 records. S2 and S3 show how 1,988 became the records here: reference drugs, single-concentration % inhibition rows, semisynthetic derivatives, plant-virus assays and one confirmed duplicate are left out.

Where a value was read

Every record has an evidence tier. Full text means the value was read in the full paper (– records). Abstract means it was read from the abstract only (–). Unclear (–) is a tier that has not been ruled on yet. Abstract values are less reliable than full-text values, so filter on the tier when it matters.

How the records were made

Four curators extracted the records from the papers assigned to them, with AI-tool assistance, and reviewed them. Papers were screened by title and abstract. This description comes from the curators and has not been independently verified; their individual statements are still pending. The outputs of the AI tools were not kept, so the extraction route cannot be rebuilt from the record.

Added by the release, not by the curators: the evidence tier, structures (InChIKey and SMILES), NCBI Taxonomy id and family, ICTV species and family, molar values (value_nM), the novelty status, the plant-provenance level and the data quality flags. The data dictionary marks every column as recorded, derived or added.

Known limits

  • Accuracy is a range, not a single figure. A benchmark of 150 records read by two independent field experts, with no third reader, puts the share of correct fields between 95.7% and 99.2% and the share of records with every checked field correct between 78.6% and 96.9% (stratified to the benchmark frame; 95% intervals in the release README, supplementary file S6). Where the two experts disagreed nobody decided between them, which is why there are two bounds. The 90% record-level target of the plan can be neither confirmed nor rejected from these data. Recall and the accuracy of compound-name resolution were not measured; the name-resolution audit was prepared but not done.
  • A unit conflict was found. On three part-2 papers the experts read a unit 1,000 times away from the recorded unit (recorded mg/mL or mM, read ug/mL or uM) for all 7 records they checked; 10 recorded potency values lie on these papers. They carry the flag unit_conflict_benchmark and should not be used until the papers are re-read.
  • Compound identity is a name lookup. About two thirds of the records have a structure, and no match was checked by hand. Near matches were rejected altogether; the 15 that were found are listed in S5.
  • Plant: some records have no plant species. For many of them a plant was found only at compound level in an external database, and that is not the plant of the assay sample. The column plant_provenance_level says which case applies.
  • Novelty is a range, not a number. The novelty status compares each record with NPASS and ChEMBL by structure, ICTV virus species and value (within 10%). The number of newly curated records is a lower bound; records that cannot be tested (no structure, no virus species, no comparable value) are counted apart. ChEMBL misses assays whose target is a host cell.
  • Open rulings: one record has an empty is_derivative and is ruled unclear; 47 workbook records have an origin (full text or abstract) that is waiting for a ruling.
  • Data quality flags mark things to re-read (possible duplicate, untagged surrogate system, value out of range or without a molecular weight, unresolved origin, unit conflict found by the benchmark). A flag does not mean a value is wrong, and a record without a flag has not been checked either. See S6 for what each flag counts.

What is not here

No NPASS or ChEMBL value (only their identifiers), no abstract or full text of any paper, no curator identifiers, and no record outside the headline scope.
