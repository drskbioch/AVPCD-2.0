Version history

0.1, 28 October 2026

  • First release: 1,731 headline-scope records from 553 papers, with evidence tier, novelty status, plant-provenance level and resolution status.
  • Compound resolution in two passes: PubChem name lookup, then PubChem synonyms, NCI CACTUS, COCONUT 2.0, LOTUS and the NPASS name table. Near matches are not accepted (ruling R002).
  • Rulings: R001 (an empty is_derivative is ruled unclear, pending adjudication) and R002 (no near matches).
  • Column data_quality_flags added to the records. The flags mark possible problems; no curated value was changed.
  • Licence: CC BY 4.0 for the curated records and derived columns.
  • Accuracy benchmark: 150 records read by two independent experts, reported as a range (see About and S6). A unit conflict on three papers is flagged (unit_conflict_benchmark).

Known gaps

  • Audit of the compound-name and plant-name resolution.
  • Rulings on the origin (full text or abstract) of 47 records.
